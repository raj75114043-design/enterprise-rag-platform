# SOP-QA-006 — Annual Product Quality Review (APQR)

**Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 2024-06-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To define the preparation and approval of the annual product quality review.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all commercial products manufactured at Meridian Pharma sites.

## 3. Responsibilities
- QA compiles the APQR.
- Production, QC and Regulatory Affairs provide inputs.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Timelines and content
The APQR shall be completed within 60 days after the end of the review period.
The review covers batches manufactured, deviations, OOS results, changes, stability data, complaints and returns.
A process capability (Cpk) below 1.33 for a critical quality attribute triggers a process review.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
