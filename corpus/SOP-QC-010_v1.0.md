# SOP-QC-010 — Audit Trail Review of Laboratory Systems

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 2025-07-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To define the frequency and method of audit trail review for laboratory computerised systems.

## 2. Scope
Applies to chromatography data systems, LIMS and standalone laboratory instruments with audit trails.

## 3. Responsibilities
- The reviewer is a trained QC analyst independent of the analyst who generated the data.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Review frequency
Audit trails of chromatography data systems shall be reviewed for every batch before release.
LIMS audit trails shall be reviewed monthly.

### 5.2 Review method
The review is documented on form QC-F-010-01.
Unexplained deletions, modifications or reprocessing are raised as deviations.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
