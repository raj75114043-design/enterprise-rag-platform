# POL-DOC-002 — Document and Record Retention Policy

**Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 2024-01-15 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To define the minimum retention periods for GMP documents and records.

## 2. Scope
Applies to paper and electronic records at all sites.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Record owners archive records.
- IT verifies backups of electronic records.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Retention periods
Batch records shall be retained for at least one year after the expiry of the batch or five years after release, whichever is longer.
Training records shall be retained for the duration of employment plus five years.

### 5.2 Electronic records
Restoration of electronic record backups shall be verified quarterly.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
