# SOP-IT-060 — Computerised System Validation and Periodic Review

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2024-10-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To define validation and periodic review of GxP computerised systems following a risk-based approach.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all GxP computerised systems, including cloud and SaaS applications.

## 3. Responsibilities
- System owners maintain the validated state.
- QA approves validation deliverables.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Periodic review
GxP-critical systems shall undergo periodic review every 2 years.
User access to GxP systems shall be reviewed quarterly.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
