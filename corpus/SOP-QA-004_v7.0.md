# SOP-QA-004 — Change Control

**Version:** 7.0 · **Effective:** 2025-04-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that changes to GMP systems, processes, equipment and documents are evaluated and approved before implementation.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all planned and emergency changes affecting GMP activities.

## 3. Responsibilities
- The change initiator raises the change request.
- Regulatory Affairs assesses regulatory impact for major changes.
- The Change Control Board approves or rejects the change.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Evaluation and approval
The Change Control Board meets weekly on Tuesdays to review open change requests.
Major changes require a regulatory impact assessment by Regulatory Affairs before approval.

### 5.2 Emergency changes
Emergency changes may be implemented first but shall be documented retrospectively within 2 working days.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
