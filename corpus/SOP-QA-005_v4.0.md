# SOP-QA-005 — Handling of Market Complaints

**Version:** 4.0 · **Effective:** 2025-03-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To ensure product complaints are recorded, investigated and responded to in a timely way.

## 2. Scope
Applies to quality complaints received from patients, healthcare professionals, distributors and regulators.

## 3. Responsibilities
- The complaint coordinator registers and acknowledges complaints.
- QA leads the investigation.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Receipt and triage
Complaints shall be acknowledged to the complainant within 2 working days.
Complaints indicating potential patient harm shall be escalated to the QA Head within 24 hours.

### 5.2 Investigation
Complaint investigations shall be closed within 30 calendar days.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
