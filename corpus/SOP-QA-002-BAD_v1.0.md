# SOP-QA-002-BAD — Deviation Management – Site Annex Baddi

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 2025-10-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** Baddi

## 1. Purpose
To record Baddi-specific requirements that modify SOP-QA-002 Deviation Management.

## 2. Scope
Applies only to the Baddi manufacturing site. Where this annex is silent, SOP-QA-002 applies.

## 3. Responsibilities
- The Baddi Site Quality Head owns this annex.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Site-specific requirements
This annex replaces the minor deviation closure timeline of SOP-QA-002 section 5.2 for the Baddi site.
At Baddi, minor deviations shall be closed within 60 calendar days of logging because of the shared QA reviewer pool.
All other deviation requirements follow the global procedure.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
