# SOP-PRD-020 — Cleaning Validation of Manufacturing Equipment

**Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 2024-08-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To establish the approach for validating cleaning procedures of shared product-contact equipment.

## 2. Scope
Applies to oral solid dosage manufacturing equipment at all sites.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Validation team prepares protocols and reports.
- QC performs swab and rinse analysis.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Acceptance criteria
The residue acceptance limit is 10 ppm or 1/1000 of the minimum therapeutic dose, whichever is lower.
Visual cleanliness is a prerequisite for swab sampling.

### 5.2 Hold times
The maximum dirty equipment hold time is 48 hours after the end of manufacturing.
The maximum clean equipment hold time is 72 hours after cleaning.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
