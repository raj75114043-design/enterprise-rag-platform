# SOP-QA-003 — Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2025-01-10 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To define how corrective and preventive actions are planned, implemented and verified for effectiveness.

## 2. Scope
Applies to CAPA arising from deviations, OOS, audits, complaints and management review.

## 3. Responsibilities
- The CAPA owner implements the action by the agreed due date.
- Quality Assurance verifies effectiveness.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Planning and implementation
Unless justified otherwise, the target completion date of a CAPA shall be 60 calendar days from approval.
A CAPA may receive one extension of up to 30 days, approved by QA with a documented justification.

### 5.2 Effectiveness check
An effectiveness check shall be performed 90 days after the CAPA is implemented.
An ineffective CAPA is re-opened and a new root-cause analysis is performed.

Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
