# SOP-ENG-030 — Calibration of Measuring Instruments

**Version:** 4.0 · **Effective:** 2025-05-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To ensure measuring instruments used in GMP activities are calibrated and fit for use.

## 2. Scope
Applies to balances, pH meters, temperature sensors and pressure gauges.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Engineering maintains the calibration schedule.
- Users perform daily verifications where required.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Calibration frequency
Analytical balances require daily verification and full calibration every 6 months.
pH meters shall be calibrated before each use with at least two buffers.

### 5.2 Out-of-tolerance results
An out-of-tolerance calibration result requires a product impact assessment within 5 working days.
