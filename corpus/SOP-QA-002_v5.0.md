# SOP-QA-002 — Deviation Management

**Version:** 5.0 · **Effective:** 2023-11-01 · **Status:** Superseded · **Site:** All sites

> This version was superseded on 2025-09-01. Refer to the current effective version.

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that unplanned departures from approved procedures are recorded, assessed, investigated and closed.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all GMP areas: manufacturing, packaging, warehouse, quality control and engineering.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Any employee who observes a deviation reports it to the area supervisor.
- The area owner performs the initial impact assessment.
- Quality Assurance classifies the deviation and approves the investigation and CAPA.

## 4. Definitions
- Deviation: any departure from an approved instruction or established standard.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Reporting and classification
Every deviation shall be logged in the electronic QMS within 24 hours of detection.
Deviations are classified as critical, major or minor based on potential impact on product quality and patient safety.
Critical deviations shall be escalated to the Site Quality Head within 4 hours.

### 5.2 Investigation and closure
Root-cause analysis uses 5-Why, fishbone or fault-tree analysis depending on complexity.
Minor deviations shall be closed within 45 calendar days of logging.
Major and critical deviations shall be closed within 45 calendar days of logging unless an extension is approved by QA.
Deviation trends shall be reviewed monthly in the Quality Review Meeting.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
