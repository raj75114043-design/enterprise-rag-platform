# SOP-TRN-050 — GMP Training

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2025-01-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To ensure employees are trained and qualified before performing GMP activities.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all employees and contractors working in GMP areas.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Department heads identify training needs.
- Training coordinators maintain training records.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Induction and refresher
New joiners shall complete GMP induction training within 30 days of joining.
GMP refresher training is conducted annually.

### 5.2 Effectiveness
The pass mark for training effectiveness assessments is 80%.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
