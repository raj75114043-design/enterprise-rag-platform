# POL-DI-001 — Data Integrity Policy

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2025-02-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To set the expectations for complete, consistent and accurate GMP data throughout its lifecycle.

## 2. Scope
Applies to paper and electronic GMP records and to every employee, contractor and system.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Every employee is accountable for the integrity of the data they create.
- System owners ensure technical controls are in place.

## 4. Definitions
- ALCOA+: the data integrity principles listed in section 5.1.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Principles
GMP data must meet ALCOA+ principles: attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, plus complete, consistent, enduring and available.
Shared or generic login credentials are strictly prohibited on GMP computerised systems.

### 5.2 Technical controls
Passwords for GMP systems shall be changed every 60 days.
Accounts shall be locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
