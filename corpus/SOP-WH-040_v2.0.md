# SOP-WH-040 — Handling of Temperature Excursions for Cold-Chain Materials

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2025-08-01 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** All sites

## 1. Purpose
To define actions when cold-chain materials (2–8 °C) are exposed to temperatures outside the labelled range.

## 2. Scope
Applies to receipt, storage and dispatch of refrigerated materials and products.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Warehouse staff download data loggers and quarantine affected material.
- QA assesses stability impact.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Detection and reporting
Any excursion above 8 °C lasting more than 30 minutes shall be reported to QA.
Data loggers shall be downloaded within 24 hours of receipt of the shipment.

### 5.2 Disposition
Affected material shall be placed in quarantine immediately until QA disposition.
QA uses stability data to decide whether the material can be released.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
