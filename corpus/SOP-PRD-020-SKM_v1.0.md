# SOP-PRD-020-SKM — Cleaning Validation – Site Annex Sikkim

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 2025-03-15 · **Status:** Effective · **Site:** Sikkim

## 1. Purpose
To record Sikkim-specific cleaning hold times that modify SOP-PRD-020.

## 2. Scope
Applies only to the Sikkim site because of high ambient humidity.

## 3. Responsibilities
- The Sikkim Validation Manager owns this annex.

## 4. Definitions
- Not applicable.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Site-specific hold times
This annex replaces the dirty hold time in SOP-PRD-020 for the Sikkim site.
At Sikkim, the maximum dirty equipment hold time is 24 hours after the end of manufacturing.

## 6. Records
Records generated under this procedure are retained as per POL-DOC-002.
